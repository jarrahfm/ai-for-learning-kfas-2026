For teachers: a simulation that collects answers

1

Build it with your AI

The prompt below makes one web page: simulation on top, your questions under it, a "Send my answers" button.

2

Collect the answers

Make a free Google Form or Microsoft Form with the same questions and paste its link into the prompt. Answers land in your spreadsheet.

3

Share one link

Click Publish or Share on the preview, choose "anyone with the link", and give students the link or a QR code.

The teacher's prompt: copy, fill the brackets, press Enter
Act as a [SUBJECT] teacher and a careful web developer. Build ONE single HTML file, no external libraries, for students aged [AGE]. Put a simulation of [CONCEPT] at the top with a slider for each thing they can change and the result shown live. Under it, put these three questions, each answered by using the sliders: [QUESTION 1] [QUESTION 2] [QUESTION 3], plus a box for the student's name. Add a big button "Send my answers" that opens this link: [PASTE YOUR FORM LINK]. Add a second button "Download my answers" that saves the name, the three answers and today's date as a text file. Keep every word simple. List your assumptions and what I should check.
No form? Students press "Download my answers" and email you the file. Want it offline? The same HTML file works from a USB stick.
