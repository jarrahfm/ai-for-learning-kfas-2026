t = 0.0 h   N = 1 cells
doubling 20 min → r = 2.08 /h
exponential alone would give 1
N(t) = K / (1 + ((K − N₀)/N₀) e−rt)
Guess first: one cell, doubling every 20 min, unlimited food. After 6 hours?
6 hours = 18 doublings, 218 = 262,144. That is why the dish fills so fast, and why nutrients become the limit.

Where you meet it

1
Lunch left on the desk at 35 °C: a few hundred cells become millions in an afternoon. That is the exponential phase you watched.
2
Finishing the antibiotic course: the slider showed that killing 90% leaves survivors that regrow. Stopping early is the same experiment on yourself.
3
Laban, yogurt and sourdough are bacteria growing on purpose, until nutrients run out: the flat part of the curve.
