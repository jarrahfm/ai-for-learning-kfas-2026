Where you meet it

1
Grandfather clocks keep time with a pendulum about one metre long: one second each way. That is why the metre and the second fit so neatly.
2
A playground swing is a pendulum. Pumping your legs changes the amplitude, not the period. A heavier friend does not swing slower.
3
Seismometers and metronomes, and Foucault's 67-metre pendulum in Paris, which showed in 1851 that the ground beneath it rotates.
