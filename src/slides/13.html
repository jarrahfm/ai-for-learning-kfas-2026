Where you meet it

1
Your phone's GPS works because satellites follow exactly this rule around the Earth: engineers choose the height to get the orbit time they want.
2
Mars missions launch only every 26 months, when Earth (1-year orbit) and Mars (1.9-year orbit) line up. The launch window is Kepler's law on a calendar.
3
The Kuwait Towers' spheres point at the sky: on a clear night Jupiter and Saturn are visible to the naked eye, moving a little each week, exactly as slowly as the slider predicts.

The prompt for this advanced view 
ROLE: You are an astronomy educator and a careful front-end developer.
TASK: Build one self-contained HTML file with a canvas that zooms continuously, with one smooth eased camera, through the real scales of the Solar System: Earth at its true diameter, Earth and Moon at their real distance, the inner planets' orbits, the outer planets, then back to the Sun at true size beside Earth.
CONTEXT: For first-year university students. Use these values and show the source on screen: planet radii (NASA JPL "Planetary Physical Parameters"), Moon 384,400 km away (NASA Moon facts), 1 AU = 149,597,870.7 km and light crosses it in 499 s (JPL "Astrodynamic Parameters"), each orbit's semimajor axis and eccentricity (JPL "Approximate Positions of the Planets"), Sun radius about 700,000 km (NASA Sun facts). Terms and definitions from OpenStax Astronomy 2e, chapter 3.
CONSTRAINTS: Everything at true scale; if a body is smaller than 3 pixels, draw a dot and say so. Real ellipses from a and e. A scale bar that switches units: km, thousand km, AU, light-minutes. Nine zoom stops, each with one term, a one-line definition and a short citation. Play/Pause, Previous/Next stop and a log-scale zoom slider. No external libraries. Do not invent numbers.
FORMAT: One HTML file with inline CSS and JS, commented for a beginner, then a table of every number used and its source.
CHECK: Before answering, confirm by hand: Jupiter's sqrt(5.203^3) = 11.87 years vs the measured 11.86; 1 AU divided by the speed of light = 8.3 minutes; the Earth-Moon distance = about 30 Earth diameters. List anything you could not source.
