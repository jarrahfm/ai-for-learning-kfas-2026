How to check anything an AI tells you

The 5-step check one sentence, five steps

“One bacterium doubling every 20 minutes becomes a million cells in two hours.”
🔍
1 · SPOT

Find the sentence that claims something.

"A million in two hours" is a claim with a number in it.
📚
2 · SOURCE

Open a real one: a textbook or a trusted website. A chatbot is not a source.

OpenStax Microbiology, "How Microbes Grow".
⚖
3 · CHECK

Compare. Does the source agree? Try one number yourself.

2 hours = 6 doublings. 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64, not a million.
🤔
4 · ASSUME

Ask: what did the AI take for granted?

It mixed up two hours with six hours (that gives 262,144).
🎨
5 · LABEL

Give it a colour, and write the source down.

UNSUPPORTED  checked by hand
Label. Your own arithmetic says it is wrong: UNSUPPORTED. Write "checked by hand, 2^6 = 64" next to it. Done in under a minute.
